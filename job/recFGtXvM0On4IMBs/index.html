仕事内容

■仕事概要
In short
You'll design and build the "curriculum" that makes Recho's AI smarter: the training and evaluation data for our LLMs, built end to end from real-world business data. Starting from call logs and our clients' business systems, you'll decide what the model should learn, build the data, and put systems in place to guarantee its quality. Today, data building is ad hoc and varies from project to project. Your job is to turn it into a repeatable, standardized workflow.
Team language:
English. Japanese at around JLPT N2 level is also required, since you'll work directly with Japanese call logs and business data.
Salary:
¥10M–¥20M per year, plus stock options
Location:
Tokyo (Hanzomon), hybrid. Candidates based outside Japan are welcome. We offer visa sponsorship and relocation support, and we're open to discussing fully remote arrangements.
You might be a fit if you are…
Someone who has built post-training data (SFT, preference, synthetic data) at a foundation model company
Someone at a customer service or conversational AI company who has designed training data from real business logs and systems
A data engineer or ML engineer who has designed and implemented data integrations with business systems yourself
About the role
At Recho, we believe the ceiling on model performance is set by the quality of rewards and data. Your core mission is to design and build LLM training and evaluation data end to end, starting from real-world data such as call logs, business systems, FAQs and manuals.
Right now, building data for each project depends on individual effort and isn't reproducible. You'll turn the whole process into a standardized system: cleansing, anonymizing and structuring raw data; building SFT and RL data (synthetic data generation, difficulty control, deduplication, mixture tuning); and maintaining evaluation sets and benchmarks.
This is the foundation for scaling to the next 20–30 client projects. You'll also design and run the "data flywheel" that turns error analysis of training results into better data. Down the line, this role could grow into leading our data and evaluation team.
What this role is
not
Not an on-call or SRE role. SLA management and infrastructure maintenance for online services are not the focus.
GPU cluster operations and general SRE/DevOps are handled by a separate team.
The final design of training algorithms and rewards (SFT/RL methods, loss functions) belongs to our LLM Training Engineer role. You'll partner with them on data and evaluation sets.
Defining client requirements and what a "correct response" looks like belongs to our Data PM role. You'll turn those definitions into real data.
What you'll do
1. Investigate, design and prepare real-world data
Investigate each project's business systems and data, and design training and evaluation data end to end
Cleanse, anonymize and structure real-world data such as call logs
2. Build training and evaluation data
Build SFT and RL data: synthetic data generation, difficulty control, deduplication and mixture tuning
Implement reward functions and evaluation sets, and maintain benchmarks for each project
3. Run the data flywheel
Design and run the loop that turns error analysis of training results into data improvements
Build data pipelines with reproducibility and versioning built in
We're happy to go into more detail during the intro call and interviews.
Why this role
Set the ceiling on model performance.
That ceiling is set by the quality of rewards and data. You'll decide what the model should learn and what belongs in the prompt, and build the foundation of its intelligence yourself.
Turn ad hoc work into a system, from zero.
You'll rebuild fragmented, project-by-project data work into a repeatable data flywheel, and design the foundation for scaling to 20–30 projects.
Work at the front line of real data and real products.
You'll work with actual call logs and business systems and turn real customer-service problems into data. This isn't clean research data; it's the data that runs production.
Tech stack
Languages:
Python (pandas, Polars, Spark, etc.)
Data infrastructure:
ETL, versioning, reproducibility, SQL, API and database integrations
Synthetic data & evaluation:
Synthetic data generation via LLM APIs, LLM-as-a-Judge
Training framework inputs:
TRL, verl (formatting SFT and RL data)
Our product
We build the Recho AI Voice Platform, which delivers voice conversations natural enough to be hard to tell apart from a human. We also deploy and operate Voice AI Agents for large enterprises.
Our edge is a platform for building and improving high-quality agents quickly. It captures industry know-how and turns user feedback into a fast, continuous improvement loop. Our agents now handle complex, large-scale contact center operations in finance, manufacturing and the public sector. In some deployments, they've resolved every call without handing off to a human agent.
